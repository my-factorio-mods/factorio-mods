Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Press Ctrl + Shift + R to fill locked (filtered) inventory slots with matching items from nearby chests.
    - Takes from the nearest chests first and matches item quality.
    - Never takes from requester or buffer chests.
    - Lists any items it couldn't fully restock in chat, with how many were missing.
    - Per-player range setting from 10 to 50 tiles.
