Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Can take from cargo landing pads, vehicle trunks (car, tank, spidertron), and cargo wagons, each with its own setting.
    - One setting per logistic chest type. Active provider, passive provider, and storage are on by default.
    - Supports quality filters like "uncommon or better".
    - With a car, tank, or spidertron window open, the hotkey refills that vehicle's locked trunk slots instead of yours.
  Changes:
    - Locked slots in vehicles and cargo wagons are treated as reserved and never taken from.
    - Does nothing while other windows (chests, machines, etc.) are open, so it never changes your inventory unexpectedly.
    - Shows a message when used without a character (remote view or a space platform) instead of doing nothing.
  Optimizations:
    - Skips chests that don't have the item instead of scanning every slot.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Press Ctrl + Shift + R to fill locked (filtered) inventory slots with matching items from nearby chests.
    - Takes from the nearest chests first and matches item quality.
    - Never takes from requester or buffer chests.
    - Lists any items it couldn't fully restock in chat, with how many were missing.
    - Per-player range setting from 10 to 50 tiles.
